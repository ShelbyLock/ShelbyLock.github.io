  1. SOUPS
    AI’ve Got a Bad Feeling About This: A Privacy Threat Modeling Framework for GenAI
    Qianying Liao, Jonah Bellemans, Laurens Sion, and 6 more authors
    In Proceedings of the Twenty-Second Symposium on Usable Privacy and Security (SOUPS ’26), Aug 2026
    Abs arXiv Bib

    As generative AI (GenAI) systems become increasingly prevalent across various technological stacks, the question of how such systems handle sensitive and personal data flows becomes increasingly important. We present an engineer-oriented privacy threat modeling framework for GenAI, built on LINDDUN and grounded in a systematic review of the GenAI privacy literature together with a hands-on analysis of a GenAI chatbot system.

2022

  1. IJCNN
    Herb: Privacy-Preserving Random Forest with Partially Homomorphic Encryption
    Qianying Liao, Bruno Cabral, João Paulo Fernandes, and 1 more author
    In 2022 International Joint Conference on Neural Networks (IJCNN), Jul 2022
    Abs DOI Bib HTML

    We address private learning and inference for the Weighted Random Forest model when data records are vertically distributed among multiple sites. We contribute efficient collaborative algorithms for computing the impurity of decision tree nodes while protecting all intermediate values, a learning protocol based on the Paillier Cryptosystem and Digital Envelopes, and an inference protocol based on Look-up Tables.

  2. PRDC
    HERB+: Evolving an Industrial-Strength Privacy-Preserving Machine Learning Framework
    Qianying Liao, Alexandre Cortez Santos, Bruno Cabral, and 2 more authors
    In 2022 IEEE 27th Pacific Rim International Symposium on Dependable Computing (PRDC), Nov 2022
    Abs Bib HTML

    HERB+ is a confidential learning framework for tree-based models under vertically dispersed data. It presents a range of privacy-preserved, distributed tree-based algorithms — Decision Tree, Random Forest, and Gradient Boosting Decision Trees for both classification and regression — and discusses the use of Fully Homomorphic Encryption during training.
