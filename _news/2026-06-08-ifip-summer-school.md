Our position paper **The Right Tool for the Job: On the Selection of Mitigations for GenAI Privacy Threats** was accepted at the [21st IFIP Summer School on Privacy and Identity Management](https://ifip-summerschool.github.io/) in Leuven. Co-first-authored with [Jonah Bellemans](https://distrinet.cs.kuleuven.be/people/JonahBellemans), who went on to win the Best Presentation award in the technical track at the school in August. :trophy:
