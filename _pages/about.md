I am a doctoral researcher at the [DistriNet](https://distrinet.cs.kuleuven.be/) Research Group, Department of Computer Science, [KU Leuven](https://www.kuleuven.be/), where I work on **Privacy by Design for Learning-based Approaches in Complex Data Ecosystems** under the supervision of Prof. Dr. [Wouter Joosen](https://distrinet.cs.kuleuven.be/people/WouterJoosen) and Prof. Dr. [Dimitri Van Landuyt](https://www.kuleuven.be/wieiswie/nl/person/00046781). From 2023 to 2025, my research focused on privacy attacks and privacy measurement techniques for synthetic data and generative models. Since 2025, I have expanded my research toward privacy risks, threat conceptualization, and evaluation methods for agentic and generative AI, including the development of a domain-specific privacy threat modeling framework for GenAI systems based on LINDDUN. More broadly, my research explores how privacy can be systematically engineered into data-driven systems by design, with recent work spanning privacy threat modeling, synthetic tabular data, and privacy-preserving and federated machine learning.
